# Hi, I’m Sayon Pramanik 👋

I am a Doctoral Researcher at **The Bartlett School of Architecture, University College London (UCL)**, working at the intersection of architecture, urban research, spatial analysis, and computational methods.

My research investigates how spatial configuration influences pedestrian movement, accessibility, collective behaviour, and spatial culture in post-colonial cities.

## Research interests

- Space Syntax
- Spatial intelligibility
- Pedestrian dynamics
- Urban morphology
- Spatial cognition
- GIS and geospatial analysis
- GeoAI and urban informatics
- Agent-based modelling
- Sustainable urban mobility
- Spatial culture and collective urban behaviour

## Current roles

- Doctoral Researcher, The Bartlett, UCL
- Associate Editor, *Cities & Health*
- Postgraduate Teaching Assistant, UCL
- Former Assistant Professor of Architecture and Planning

## Current work

I am currently exploring:

- pedestrian movement and mass urban gatherings;
- processional routes and spatial culture;
- inherited urban structures in post-colonial cities;
- spatial network analysis and accessibility;
- computational approaches to urban movement;
- historical, cultural, and morphological readings of cities.
